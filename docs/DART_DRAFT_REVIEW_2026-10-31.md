## Material errors requiring holds

- Donation and investment decisions are sometimes summarized as completed transfers. One draft uses a board-resolution date as the donation date even though payment is scheduled over five years.
- A buyback-resolution date is incorrectly copied from a separate newspaper-report date; an audit-attendance count is inferred from the independent-director count.
- A filing contact’s department and title are assigned to the reporting executive. A different draft mistakes a general 2003 appointment field for appointment as chairman; [Samsung’s official announcement](https://news.samsung.com/global/jay-y-lee-appointed-samsung-electronics-executive-chairman) dates that chair appointment to 27 October 2022.
- Several drafts omit material provisional, scheduled, fees-excluded, reference-price or account-scope qualifications. Some quotations stop before the unit/audit clause required to support the attached claim.
